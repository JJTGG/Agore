## 1. Introduction

Agoré ("Agoré", "we", "us", or "our") is a social platform designed to help people connect, share content, discover others, and communicate.

This Privacy Policy explains how Agoré collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when people use the platform.

It also explains the choices and rights users may have regarding their personal information.

Agoré is under active development. Some features, settings, and procedures may change as the platform evolves. Material changes to our privacy practices will be reflected in this Policy, with notice provided where required by applicable law.

## 3. Information we collect

The information we process depends on how a person uses Agoré, which features are enabled, and the permissions they grant.

### 3.1 Account and authentication information

When an account is created or used, Agoré may process:

- Email address.
- Username and display name.
- Internal account and profile identifiers.
- Authentication and session-related information.
- Account status and relevant account-verification state.
- Authentication-provider information where an external sign-in provider is used.
- Information required to complete onboarding or validate an invitation while invite-only access is enabled.

Authentication credentials are handled through the configured authentication system. Agoré does not require users to provide their account passwords to support staff.

### 3.2 Profile information

Users may provide profile information such as:

- Profile photo or avatar.
- Biography.
- Optional location displayed on their profile.
- Optional profile links.
- Other profile details made available by the application.

Users should avoid including sensitive personal information in public-facing profile fields.

Information displayed on a profile may be visible to other users according to the application's functionality and applicable access controls.

### 3.3 Content and interactions

When users participate in Agoré, the platform may process:

- Posts and associated media.
- Comments and replies.
- Reactions and reposts.
- Follow and block relationships.
- Content identifiers and timestamps.
- Records associated with reporting or moderation.
- Other information needed to provide social and discovery features.

Content visibility depends on how the relevant feature operates and the access controls applied to it.

Other users may be able to view, save, capture, or redistribute content they are permitted to access. Removing content from Agoré cannot guarantee that independently saved copies disappear.

### 3.4 Messages and conversations

Agoré supports direct conversations and group conversations.

Depending on the features used, message-related information may include:

- Message text.
- Voice messages and other supported attachments.
- Media files and associated metadata.
- Conversation membership and administrative roles.
- Message replies and reactions.
- Message timestamps and read-status information.
- Information required to display conversation lists and message previews.

Private conversations are intended to be accessible to their authorised participants.

Access may also be necessary in limited circumstances to operate or secure the service, investigate abuse or a user-submitted report, respond to a legal obligation, or provide requested support. Such access must be controlled and limited to what is appropriate for the purpose.

Users should not assume that information shared through any online service is immune from legal disclosure, security incidents, or access by an unintended recipient.

### 3.5 Notifications and push subscriptions

If users enable supported notification features, Agoré may process:

- Notification records and event timestamps.
- Notification preferences.
- Browser push subscription endpoints.
- Browser push encryption-related subscription keys.
- Information needed to associate a subscription with the relevant account.
- Delivery status or error information where available.

Push notifications may appear on a device's lock screen or notification panel. Their visibility can depend on the operating system, browser, and device settings.

Users can manage browser notification permissions through their browser or device settings. Where supported by the application, users may also remove a push subscription through Agoré's notification settings.

### 3.6 Reports and support requests

When users submit reports or contact support, Agoré may process:

- The report or support request itself.
- The account associated with the request.
- Relevant content identifiers and supporting information.
- Messages exchanged with support.
- Case status, priority, assignment, and timestamps.
- Internal case-management records needed to resolve the request or maintain an appropriate audit trail.

Information supplied in a report or support request may be available to authorised personnel responsible for handling that matter.

Please avoid including passwords, authentication tokens, payment credentials, or unrelated sensitive personal information in support requests.

### 3.7 Technical, diagnostic, and security information

Agoré and its infrastructure providers may process technical information needed to operate, maintain, and secure the service, including:

- Request and authentication event information.
- Error and diagnostic information.
- Timestamps and information about application activity.
- Rate-limit and abuse-prevention records.
- Browser, device, network, or connection information made available through normal service requests or provider logs.
- Information required to investigate service failures, suspicious activity, or security incidents.

The exact information available depends on the relevant component and its configuration. Before publication, Agoré must confirm what its application, hosting provider, database provider, and authentication system actually collect and retain.

## 4. How we use personal information

Agoré may use personal information for the following purposes.

### 4.1 Providing the service

We process information to:

- Create and maintain accounts.
- Authenticate users and manage sessions.
- Complete onboarding.
- Display profiles and content.
- Support following, blocking, comments, reactions, and reposts.
- Deliver private and group conversations.
- Provide notifications and other requested features.
- Store and display supported media.
- Maintain user settings.

### 4.2 Maintaining security and integrity

We may process information to:

- Prevent unauthorised access.
- Detect abuse, spam, suspicious behaviour, and technical attacks.
- Enforce account restrictions and platform policies.
- Investigate reports and security incidents.
- Protect users, staff, and the platform.
- Maintain appropriate records of privileged actions and relevant operational events.

### 4.3 Providing support

We use relevant information to respond to account problems, resolve technical issues, investigate user reports, and manage support cases.

### 4.4 Operating and improving Agoré

We may use appropriate operational information to diagnose errors, investigate reliability problems, understand whether features are functioning correctly, and improve the service.

Any additional analytics or product-measurement practices must be assessed and disclosed appropriately before implementation.

### 4.5 Meeting legal obligations

We may process or disclose information when necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, protect legal rights, or address emergencies as permitted by law.

## 5. Legal bases for processing

Where applicable data-protection law requires a lawful basis, Agoré will identify and rely on a basis appropriate to the processing activity.

Depending on the circumstances and jurisdiction, this may include:

- **Contract or requested service:** processing necessary to create an account and provide requested platform features.
- **Legitimate interests:** processing reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, service reliability, and protection of users, where those interests are not overridden by applicable rights.
- **Consent:** processing for which valid consent is required or relied upon, including applicable optional permissions or technologies.
- **Legal obligation:** processing necessary to comply with binding legal requirements.
- **Other legally recognised bases:** where available and appropriate under the law that applies.

The precise legal basis may differ between activities and jurisdictions.

Where processing relies on consent, users may withdraw that consent as permitted by applicable law. Withdrawal does not automatically make earlier lawful processing invalid, and it may affect the availability of a feature that depends on the relevant permission.

The final published Policy must be checked against the actual purposes, configurations, and legal bases used by Agoré.

## 6. Public content and private information

Agoré includes social features through which users share information with other people.

Information submitted to public-facing features may be visible to other users according to the relevant feature and its access controls. Users should consider the audience of content before publishing it.

Private conversations are distinct from public posts. Access to private conversations must be restricted according to the applicable membership and authorisation rules.

Information users voluntarily submit to a report or support request may be reviewed by authorised personnel who need it to handle the request.

Agoré must not treat information as public merely because it is stored in the application database. Database storage and access permissions do not, by themselves, determine the audience intended for a piece of information.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

Agoré uses necessary session mechanisms and may use related browser storage or service-worker functionality to provide requested features.

These technologies may be used to:

- Maintain authentication sessions.
- Support secure account flows.
- Complete invitation validation while private access is enabled.
- Maintain relevant application functionality.
- Support notification features where enabled.

Browsers and infrastructure providers may also process technical information as part of normal service operation.

The current V0 product is not intended to use personal information for behavioural advertising. This statement must be verified against actual application code, provider settings, and operational practices before publication.

If Agoré introduces optional analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking technologies, it will assess the applicable consent and disclosure requirements and update its practices accordingly.

Users can manage relevant browser settings, although disabling necessary session or storage functionality may prevent some features from working correctly.

## 8. When we share information

Agoré does not make personal information generally available to third parties simply because those parties request it.

Information may be disclosed in the following circumstances.

### 8.1 Other users

Information may be visible to other users where it is part of a profile, post, interaction, conversation, or other feature they are authorised to access.

### 8.2 Service providers

Agoré relies on external infrastructure to operate the platform.

Depending on the service and feature, providers may process relevant information on our behalf. These may include:

- **Supabase:** authentication, database, storage, and related backend services.
- **Vercel:** application hosting, deployment, and associated operational services.
- **Authentication providers:** where Google, Apple, or another external provider is enabled for sign-in.
- **Browser and push infrastructure:** where web-push notifications are enabled.

The providers involved, data they process, applicable contractual terms, and relevant data-processing arrangements must be reviewed before public launch.

Service providers should receive only the information reasonably necessary for their service and should be subject to appropriate contractual, technical, or legal safeguards.

### 8.3 Legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information when required or permitted by applicable law, or when reasonably necessary to address a serious security incident, protect users, investigate abuse, establish or defend legal claims, or protect the rights and safety of people or the platform.

### 8.4 Business changes

If Agoré undergoes a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of relevant operations, personal information may be involved in that process where legally permitted. Appropriate safeguards and notices will be provided where required.

### 8.5 No unrestricted disclosure

A request from a third party does not automatically authorise disclosure. Requests must be assessed against the relevant legal obligations, user rights, and access controls.

## 9. Sale and advertising

Before publication, the operator must verify that actual product behaviour, provider configurations, and any existing commercial arrangements support this statement.

If Agoré later introduces advertising, data sharing for advertising, or other materially different uses of personal information, it must assess applicable legal requirements, update the Policy, and provide any required notices and choices before implementing those practices.

## 11. Data retention

Agoré retains personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Retention may depend on the type of information and why it was collected.

Examples include:

- Account and profile information needed to provide an active account.
- Posts, comments, and media retained to provide the features users request.
- Conversation records retained to support message history and associated functionality.
- Support and reporting records retained to address requests, investigate incidents, and maintain appropriate operational records.
- Security and diagnostic records retained for an appropriate period to detect abuse and maintain service reliability.
- Backups retained according to the applicable backup and recovery schedule.

Some information may need to be retained for a longer period when required by law, necessary to establish or defend legal claims, or needed for a legitimate security purpose.

Before publication, Agoré must define and document actual retention periods or meaningful retention criteria for the major categories of information.

## 13. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These rights may include:

- Requesting access to personal information we hold about you.
- Requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Requesting deletion of personal information where legally available.
- Requesting restriction of certain processing.
- Objecting to certain processing where applicable.
- Withdrawing consent where processing relies on consent.
- Requesting a portable copy of certain information.
- Complaining to the relevant data-protection authority.
- Exercising additional rights available under applicable local law.

These rights are not absolute in every circumstance. Some requests may be subject to lawful exceptions, and the rights available differ between jurisdictions.

### How to submit a request

Email **agore.support@gmail.com** with the subject line:

`Agoré Privacy Request`

Describe the request and, where possible, identify the account to which it relates.

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity and protect the account from unauthorised disclosure.

Agoré will assess and respond to privacy requests within the period required by applicable law.

We will not require more personal information for verification than is reasonably necessary for the request.

### Complaints

If you believe your personal information has been handled unlawfully, contact us first so the matter can be reviewed.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority in your jurisdiction.

For users in Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission is the country's data-protection authority.

## 14. Security

Agoré uses technical and organisational controls intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Relevant controls may include:

- Authentication and session management.
- Database access policies.
- Server-side authorisation checks.
- Restricted access to privileged operations.
- Rate limiting and abuse prevention.
- Controlled media access.
- Security logging and incident investigation.
- Access restrictions for support and staff workflows.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their credentials, avoid sharing account access, and report suspected account compromise promptly.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately according to the process described in `SECURITY.md`.
